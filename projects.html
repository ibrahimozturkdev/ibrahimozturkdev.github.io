Side Projects & Experiments

Prototypes, jam entries, and personal work — outside of studio hours.

City Builder / Strategy
2026

Layer Kingdom

Layer Kingdom is a strategy city-building game that combines complex city management mechanics with a unique layered world. Rather than being limited to a traditional side-scrolling experience, players can expand their kingdom both horizontally and across multiple depth layers, adding a new strategic dimension to city planning and resource management. The game is inspired by Kingdom: Two Crowns and Frostpunk.

Developed in Unreal Engine 5 as my university capstone project, the game was designed and built over the course of three months by a two-person team. I was responsible for the game's core design, gameplay systems, and technical art implementation.

CO-OP Shooter
2024

Reflect_On

Reflect_On is a competitive CO-OP game inspired by classic arcade titles. Two players face off in an arena where walls are not obstacles, but tools. Every surface can reflect bullets, allowing players to use ricochets to outsmart and eliminate their opponent. The first player to lose all their health is defeated.

Developed in Unreal Engine 5, the game was created by a six-person team for BUG Spring Jam 2024.

Top-down & TPS
2023

Frozen Cortex

Frozen Cortex is a 3D pixelated shooter set in a dystopian Cold War world. Players take control of a Soviet soldier whose perception has been altered by a mind-control chip, causing innocent civilians to appear as zombies. As the truth begins to unfold, players fight their way through a metro station in a short, atmospheric thriller experience. Inspired by retro shooters, the game features both top-down and third-person perspectives.

Developed in Unreal Engine 5, the game was created by a team of 5 for Mağara Jam 2023.

Isometric / Tycoon
2021

Indie Dev Tycoon

Indie Dev Tycoon is an isometric simulation game where players take on the role of an indie game developer, balancing game development with their personal well-being. Each playthrough challenges players to create better games while managing limited time, energy, and life responsibilities.

Developed in Unreal Engine 4, the game was created as a solo project in just 48 hours for a game jam.

Tower Defense / Mobile
2020

Defend The Village

Defend the Village is a simple tower defense game developed for mobile platforms, where players place and upgrade defensive towers to protect their village from waves of incoming enemies.

Developed in Unreal Engine 4, the game was built over two months in 2020 and published on the Google Play Store. I documented the entire development process on my YouTube channel.
